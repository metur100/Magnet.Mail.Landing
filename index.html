About the game

The physics puzzle game where magnets do the delivering

Magnet Mail is a free physics puzzle game for Android and iPhone. Your job is simple: get the parcel into the right mailbox. The catch: you never touch it. Hold the blue ATTRACT button and the parcel flies towards the blue magnet; tap the red REPEL button and it is pushed away from the red one. Short taps give quick impulses, long holds give steady force. Combine them to launch parcels over walls, slingshot them around obstacles and bounce them between magnetic fields.

30 hand-made levels take you through six worlds: a quiet neighbourhood, busy city streets, a railway district with speeding trains, an industrial zone full of conveyor belts and gears, an airport with jet streams and timed doors, and a space station with low gravity, magnetic storms and wormholes. Every world adds a new parcel with its own physics. Light envelopes fly fast, heavy crates need strong fields, glass breaks on hard hits and bouncy packages spring off every wall.

Levels are short, so it's the perfect brain teaser for a quick break. Earn three stars with fewer impulses, fewer bumps and time to spare, watch the replay of your route, and come back for a new Daily Delivery every day. The game is suitable for kids and adults and fair by design: no ads, no in-app purchases, no account and no tracking. It plays fully offline.

Support

Need a hand?

Answers to the most common questions. Still stuck? Just write.

I'm stuck on a level. Is there a hint?

Yes. After a failed attempt choose Retry with a hint: the game shows a working route with every ATTRACT and REPEL press marked. Hints are free.

Why did my parcel bounce out of the mailbox?

Either it was the wrong mailbox (check the address – wrong ones are marked with an ✕), or the parcel came in too fast. Release ATTRACT a little earlier so it slows down.

How do I get three stars?

Deliver with no more impulses than the level's par, few collisions, no wrong mailbox and at least 30 % of the time left.

I lost my progress. Can you restore it?

Progress is stored only on your device. We never receive it, so it can't be restored after uninstalling the game or resetting progress.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Everything, including the Daily Delivery, works fully offline.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. The game is completely free.

How do I delete my data?

Use Settings → Reset progress in the game, or uninstall it. Nothing is stored anywhere else. See the privacy policy.
